Troubleshooting

Common errors candidates hit in the app, and what to tell them.

  • No Internet Connection

    Ask the candidate to check their connection, hardwire if possible, and relaunch the app.

  • Email Addresses Do Not Match

    The app shows "The email addresses do not match!" when the two email boxes differ. Ask the candidate to type the same address in both, using the one that received the invitation. Capital letters and extra spaces do not matter.

  • Error Submitting

    The app shows "We could not send your results. Please try again." when the results could not be saved. Ask the candidate to check their connection and press Submit again. They do not need to rerun the scan.

Version History

Changes to the app candidates download from this page.

Version 1.3 · October 22, 2026

  • The results now include each monitor's screen size. Some clients state a minimum, and the app could not report it before.
  • Results are now labelled with the tool that produced them, so a desktop run is never confused with the browser check.

Version 1.2 · October 22, 2026

  • New look, matching Windows 11 rather than the older grey styling.
  • The download is about a third of the size, so it arrives faster.
  • Antivirus is now detected correctly. Earlier versions could report "Windows Defender" or "None" on a machine running Avast, Norton or similar.
  • The network check reports whether Wi-Fi is still switched on alongside a wired connection, which some clients require to be off.
  • The results screen now shows the camera, microphone and antivirus readings, which were previously collected but not displayed.
  • Messages no longer get cut off in the smaller windows.

Version 1.1 · October 21, 2026

  • Results now save directly to Paramount's systems. The email address no longer has to match an existing record before the scan can start.
  • Candidates type their email address twice to catch typos.
  • The app only reports "Submitted Successfully" once the results are saved, and shows an error the candidate can retry if they are not.

Version 1.0 · June 26, 2026

  • Rebuilt on Python 3.13, with more reliable network checks and total RAM reporting.

Privacy Policy

Our commitment to your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy explains how Paramount Computer Diagnostic collects, uses, and protects information.

Information Collection and Use

Paramount Computer Diagnostic collects data from your device to assess job compatibility and update your Bullhorn profile. The specific information collected includes:

  • System Hardware Details: Windows version, machine type, active monitors and their screen sizes, free and total RAM, CPU type, processor, cores, hard-drive availability, and antivirus information.
  • Network Information: Download and upload speeds, network connection type, and network provider.
  • Audio and Video Input Devices: Microphone availability and camera availability.
  • Bullhorn Integration: Collected data may be integrated into your Bullhorn profile using the email address registered with Paramount.

Information Sharing and Disclosure

The collected data is used to evaluate candidate eligibility for specific job positions and is not shared with third parties outside the organization, except as necessary to fulfill job-related requests.

Access to Your Information and Control

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your information. Contact support@hireparamount.com to manage your data.

Security Measures

While we employ security measures to protect your information, avoid transmitting sensitive data through email to mitigate potential risks.
