Paramount Computer Diagnostic.

Check that your computer and internet are ready for your work-from-home job. It takes about 2 minutes and nothing stays installed.

Download

For Windows 10 and 11. Your browser or Windows may warn you about a downloaded program. That is normal: choose Keep, or More info and then Run anyway.

Paramount Computer Diagnostics

Version 1.7.1 · Last updated: November 6, 2026

The check takes about 2 minutes once the app is open. It looks at your processor, memory, disk, screens, camera, microphone, antivirus and Windows Update, and tests your internet.

  1. Download the app and open it.
  2. Type your email address twice. Use the address you applied with.
  3. If your recruiter emailed you a code, type it in the Recruiter code box. Otherwise leave it blank.
  4. Press Start and keep the window open while it checks.
  5. If anything needs fixing, the app says what and how. Fix what you can, press Check again, then press Send results. You can close the app after that.

Is this safe? The app is signed by Paramount Staffing, LLC, so Windows names us as the publisher. It runs once and leaves nothing running. It needs no administrator rights and does not read your files, browsing, messages or passwords. It saves only your recruiter code on your computer. The Privacy Policy below lists what it reads and what it sends. Your results are saved with Paramount only when you press Send results. If you entered a recruiter code, your readings go to our readiness service first to build your checklist, and are not saved there.

Will it run on my computer? It needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. It will not open on 32-bit Windows or in Windows S mode: to leave S mode, open Settings, System, Activation, and switch out of S mode, which is free. On an ARM laptop it runs, but many work programs do not, so ask your recruiter. On a Mac or Chromebook, use the browser check instead, then tell your recruiter which computer you have.

Help

What the app's messages mean and what to do. If you are still stuck, ask your recruiter.

  • Windows Protected Your PC

    Windows can show this for a new program. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The publisher shown is Paramount Staffing, LLC.

  • We Could Not Reach the Internet

    Check your connection, plug in a network cable if you can, and press Start again.

  • The Two Email Addresses Do Not Match

    Type the same address in both boxes. Capital letters and extra spaces do not matter.

  • We Do Not Recognise That Code

    Check the code in your recruiter's email and type it again. If it still does not work, leave the box blank, run the check, and tell your recruiter.

  • Something Went Wrong While Checking

    Press Check again. If it happens again, ask your recruiter.

  • We Could Not Send Your Results

    Check your internet and press Send results again. You do not need to run the check again.

Privacy Policy

Our commitment to your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy explains how Paramount Computer Diagnostic collects, uses, and protects information.

Information Collection and Use

Paramount Computer Diagnostic collects data from your device to check it against the requirements of the work-at-home job you applied for. Results are saved to Paramount's own systems under the email address you enter, and may later be added to your candidate record with us. Press "See what we found" before you send to see the main readings. The app also sends the other details listed here. Your results are saved only when you press Send results. Before that, a short note says a check started or stopped, and if you entered a code your readings are checked by our readiness service. The specific information collected includes:

  • System Hardware Details: Windows version, edition and build, whether it is 64-bit and activated, machine type and model, active monitors with their screen sizes, free, installed and usable RAM, CPU type, processor, cores, disks (size, free space, SSD or hard drive), graphics, how busy the processor is, how long since the computer started, whether it is on battery, whether it has a TPM security chip and which version, and the display language of Windows.
  • Security and Updates: Installed antivirus products and whether each is switched on and up to date, when Windows Defender last scanned, whether Windows Update is on and when it last installed an update, firewall, disk encryption, Secure Boot, and how soon the screen locks when left.
  • Setup: Whether your account can install software, and whether the computer is managed by a work or school.
  • Network Information: Download and upload speeds, ping, jitter and packet loss, network connection type (wired, Wi-Fi, cellular), whether the Wi-Fi radio is on, whether a VPN or proxy is in use, how fast the network adapter links, network provider, public IP address, the country, state and time zone that address belongs to, and your computer's time zone offset. We collect the state because some clients can only hire in certain states. An internet address shows only roughly where you are, so a recruiter reviews it.
  • Audio and Video Devices: Microphone, camera, headset, keyboard and mouse availability, and whether they connect by USB or Bluetooth.
  • Selected Software: Installed web browsers and their versions, installed remote-access software, whether a known remote-access tool is running now, and whether this is a remote desktop session. The app does not list other software, and it does not send a list of running programs.
  • About the Run: The app version, a random number for each run, how long each step took, and any errors. A short note with your email address is sent when a check starts, and when it stops early, so we can see where people get stuck.

The app does not read your files, browsing history, messages, passwords, or Wi-Fi network names.

Information Sharing and Disclosure

The collected data is used to evaluate candidate eligibility for specific job positions and is not shared with third parties outside the organization, except as necessary to fulfill job-related requests.

Access to Your Information and Control

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your information. Contact support@hireparamount.com to manage your data.

Security Measures

While we employ security measures to protect your information, avoid transmitting sensitive data through email to mitigate potential risks.

Links to Other Sites

Paramount Computer Diagnostic contains one link to an external site: the Help section of this page, maintained by Paramount.

If you enter a code from your recruiter, the code is saved with your results, and your readings are sent to Paramount's readiness service (readiness.hireparamount.com) to check them against the job's requirements. The code is also remembered on your computer so you do not have to type it again.

To measure your connection the app exchanges test data with Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com and 1.1.1.1), pings Google (8.8.8.8), and looks up your network provider with ipinfo.io. Those services see your public IP address, as any website you visit does.
